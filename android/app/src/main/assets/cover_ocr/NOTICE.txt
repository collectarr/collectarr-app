Cover text recognition models

det.onnx   PP-OCRv4 mobile text detection, by the PaddleOCR authors
           (ch_PP-OCRv4_det_infer.onnx from rapidocr_onnxruntime 1.3)
rec.onnx   PP-OCRv5 mobile Latin text recognition, by the PaddleOCR authors
           (latin_PP-OCRv5_rec_mobile.onnx from RapidOCR 3.10.0)
keys.txt   The character list stored in rec.onnx's metadata.

The models come unchanged from RapidOCR's ONNX conversions and are licensed
under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (LICENSE.txt).
